௩ · Private by design

உங்க குரல், உங்க கணினியிலேயே.Your voice never leaves your computer.

Mozhi runs the open Whisper speech model through whisper.cpp, right on your machine. On Apple Silicon it uses the GPU through Metal. There is no sign-in, no server and no subscription.

Want tidier punctuation? Switch on polish and a small language model running locally through Ollama cleans up the text. That stays on your machine too, and if it's slow or unsure, Mozhi pastes the plain transcript instead.

After pasting, Mozhi puts your old clipboard back.

THE STORY OF

ஆய்தம்Āytham

ஃ is a letter no other script has: three dots with a sound of their own. It's our logo, and it's the pill that listens to you, rising and falling with your voice.

மூவேந்தர்The three crowned kings

The Chera bow, the Chola tiger and the Pandya fish stand for the three dynasties that carried Tamil for two thousand years. Three dots, three crowns.

புள்ளிPulli

Every kolam starts as a grid of dots on the doorstep at dawn, and then one unbroken line weaves around them. In the same way, every word starts as a sound.

தஞ்சை · 1010Thanjavur, 1010 CE

கல்லில் எழுதிய மொழி.A language written in stone.

Rajaraja Chola's Big Temple at Thanjavur, finished in 1010 CE, carries long Tamil inscriptions on its walls, recording gifts, dancers, lamps and land. A thousand years later, Mozhi writes Tamil in pixels.

௭ · Questions

கேள்விகள்Questions, answered.

இது இலவசமா? Is it free?

Yes. Mozhi is free and open source under Apache-2.0. There's no account, subscription or paid tier.

இணையம் வேண்டுமா? Does it need the internet?

Only once, to download a speech model (about 550 MB for the recommended one). After that it works fully offline.

எந்த app-ல வேலை செய்யும்? Which apps does it work in?

Anything you can type into: Slack, WhatsApp Desktop, Gmail in your browser, Notes, Word, VS Code and more. It pastes just as you would.

ஏன் Accessibility அனுமதி? Why does macOS ask for Accessibility access?

Mozhi needs it to notice the double-tap, see which app and field you're in, and paste. It never records your typing: the listener only watches for the trigger key, plus Enter and Esc while the pill is open.

தமிழ் எவ்வளவு சரியா வரும்? How accurate is the Tamil?

Large Whisper models handle clear Tamil speech well. Heavy background noise, strong accents and rare names can still trip them up. For better accuracy, pick a larger model or try a Tamil fine-tune.

இலங்கை, மலேசியத் தமிழ்? Sri Lankan or Malaysian Tamil?

It works, but accuracy varies by accent. Tell us how it does for you; dialect test reports help a lot.

Windows, Linux? Which platforms?

macOS 12+ (Apple Silicon and Intel) and Windows 10/11 today. A Linux port would make a great first contribution.

Polish என்ன செய்யும்? What does the optional polish do?

It fixes punctuation and spacing and keeps English words in English. It never translates or adds anything. It runs on a local model through Ollama, and if that model is slow or unsure, you get the plain transcript.
