கருThe idea

Every word starts as a dot.

The mark is ஃ, āytham, a letter only Tamil has. Its three dots carry three meanings at once, and the product uses all three.

ஃ

ஆய்தம்

The letter. Three pulli in a triangle, used in words like எஃகு (steel). It is the logo, the app icon and the menu-bar icon.

மூவேந்தர்

The three crowned kingdoms: Chera, Chola and Pandya. Three dots, three kings, one language. Their emblems make up the brand's graphic set.

புள்ளிக் கோலம்

The dot grid of a kolam, drawn at every doorstep each morning. One unbroken line around every dot is how Mozhi draws its patterns.

அசைவுMotion

Three dots that listen

The dots are the only thing that moves. They follow your voice while you speak, gather into ஃ while Mozhi writes, and settle when the text lands.

IdleRow, gap 1.6 × diameter. Breathes 1 → 1.08 every 2.4 s.
Listeningy = −A · sin(2π · 1.6 Hz · t − 0.9 · i). A runs from 2 px up to 1.1 × diameter with voice level, with a fast attack and a slow release.
WritingGlide into ஃ, 420 ms, cubic-bezier(.34,1.56,.64,1). One turn every 1.2 s inside the prabha at 30% opacity.
DoneUpright ஃ, one 360 ms pulse to 1.28×. "✓ Inserted into App" appears.
ErrorThree shakes of 6 px over 420 ms, tinted kumkum 300.
Reduced motionStatic positions only. State is carried by colour and label.

குரல்Voice

Talk like a friend from home

Tamil leads and English supports. Use spoken Tamil, the way people actually type, with English words left in English when that is how people say them.

Say

  • பேசுங்கள் — தமிழ், Tanglish, English…Plain invitation. The three modes are named the way users name them.
  • Slack-இல் ஒட்டப்பட்டதுSays what happened and where.
  • மைக்ரோஃபோன் தடுக்கப்பட்டுள்ளது — Settings-இல் அனுமதி கொடுங்கள்The problem, then the fix.

Avoid

  • ஒலிவாங்கி உள்ளீட்டுச் சாதனம்Formal coinages nobody says aloud. Use "மைக்ரோஃபோன்".
  • ஏதோ தவறு நடந்தது!Vague and alarming. Name the cause.
  • AI-powered magic ✨Hype. Mozhi is a tool, and it is honest about what runs where.
